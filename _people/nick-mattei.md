Nick Mattei is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Tulane University. His research covers the theory and practice of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science, and the impact of these technologies on society. He builds algorithms, mechanisms, and systems with stakeholders at the individual, local, and national levels, using theory, data, and behavioral experiments to help people and autonomous agents make better, more equitable decisions.

He is the elected chair of ACM SIGAI, founder and maintainer of [PrefLib.org](https://preflib.org), co-founder and co-director of the Tulane Center for Community Engaged Artificial Intelligence, and the inaugural Associate Director of Artificial Intelligence for Science at the Tulane AI Center. He also holds a secondary appointment at the University of Bergen.

Before Tulane he was a Research Staff Member at IBM Research and a researcher at Data61/NICTA in Sydney. He holds a PhD from the University of Kentucky. He is a co-author of the MIT Press textbook *Computing and Technology Ethics: Engaging through Science Fiction* and a 2024 NSF CAREER awardee.
