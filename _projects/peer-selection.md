How should a group of peers, like reviewers at a conference, pick the best among themselves? This project, with Omer Lev at Ben-Gurion University, develops metrics for judging peer selection mechanisms, distributed mechanisms that need no central controller, ways to handle reviews that carry over between venues, and incentives for reviewers to put in effort without letting anyone affect their own chance of being selected.
