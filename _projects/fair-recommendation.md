Fairness-aware recommendation algorithms that treat recommendation as a multi-agent problem of allocation and aggregation. With CU Boulder and the non-profit Kiva.org, the team interviews stakeholders to learn how fairness works in practice inside an organization, then deploys and evaluates a multi-stakeholder fairness solution.
