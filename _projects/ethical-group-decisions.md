title: Modeling and Learning Ethical Principles for Group Decision Support Systems
thrust: decisions
funder: NSF RI
years: 2021–2023
order: 5

Mechanisms that bound the decisions AI systems can make, so that group decisions stay aligned with human values. Taking inspiration from how people constrain their choices by moral, ethical, or business priorities, the project develops formal ways to embed ethics into AI agents and group decision support systems. With K. Brent Venable (IHMC) and Lirong Xia (RPI).
