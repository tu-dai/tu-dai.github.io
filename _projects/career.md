title: "Making Better Decisions: Human-Centered Computational Social Choice"
thrust: decisions
funder: NSF CAREER
years: 2024–2029
order: 1
---
Building the systems and algorithms needed for better decisions in online, deliberative, and interactive settings, in ways that align with human values and stay under human control. The work develops new data, models, and metrics of people's preferences, and new mechanisms for human-centered social choice. Code and data are released through PrefLib, and service-learning students work with local non-profits on the project.
