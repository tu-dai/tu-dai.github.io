The center's starting point is that AI will only succeed with the trust of the communities it affects, especially historically underserved groups. That trust has to be earned with systems that are fair, transparent, and accountable, that engage communities from design through deployment, and that support human interaction rather than replace it. Co-directed by Aron Culotta and Nick Mattei, with partners in public health, sociology, economics, and Africana studies.
