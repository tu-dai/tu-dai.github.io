{%- comment -%} Prints `authors` with lab members bolded and linked to their People page. A member is matched by their `name`, or by every spelling in their `pub_names` list. Set `authors` before including this file. Variables here start with "bold_" because includes share variables with the page that includes them; plain names like `person` would overwrite the page's own. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign bold_authors = authors -%} {%- for bold_member in site.people -%} {%- if bold_member.pub_names -%} {%- assign bold_spellings = bold_member.pub_names -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign bold_spellings = bold_member.name | split: "|" -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign bold_href = bold_member.url | relative_url -%} {%- for bold_spelling in bold_spellings -%} {%- assign bold_tagged = '' | append: bold_spelling | append: "" -%} {%- assign bold_authors = bold_authors | replace: bold_spelling, bold_tagged -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%} {{ bold_authors }}
